# M9.2 — REQ-YOLO quantization (ADMM, powers of 2)

Goal: reproduce the quantization of 2019-ding on Tiny-YOLOv2: 6-bit "mixed powers-of-two"
weights, whose multiplication reduces to two shifts and an addition [2019-ding#008.1],
obtained by ADMM [2019-ding#009.1].

### [x] T9.2.1 — Projectors onto the allowed levels
- **Spec**: §9.2 (REQ-YOLO) · **Depends on**: T4.2 · **Size**: S
- **Deliverables**: `python/yolo/quant/pow2.py` + tests
- **Acceptance**: the projection returns the nearest level (equidistant and powers of 2);
  product by two shifts and an addition == integer product
- **Notes**: α per output channel: the structure survives BN folding (α'_f = s_f·α_f)
- **Done**: `pow2.py` (`mixed6`, `uniform6`, `pot5`, `int8`; α per channel over 32
  scales; codes (sign, a, k)); `test_pow2.py`.

### [x] T9.2.2 — Powers-of-2 PTQ
- **Spec**: §9.2 · **Depends on**: T9.2.1, T4.5 · **Size**: S
- **Acceptance**: VOC2007 test mAP of the projected Darknet weights without retraining
  (floor for T9.2.3)
- **Done**: `tools/quant_lowbit.py --weights pow2` (calibrated INT8 activations): mixed6
  **52.46**, uniform6 54.35 (INT8: 55.66).

### [ ] T9.2.3 — ADMM
- **Spec**: §9.2 · **Depends on**: T9.2.1, T9.3.3 · **Size**: L
- **Deliverables**: `python/yolo/train/admm.py`, `tools/train.py --admm`
- **Acceptance**: ‖W − Z‖ → 0 over the iterations; mAP after the final projection
- **PC profiles**: ρ grid / Z-U step and non-convergence diagnosis: [T12.6](M12-profils-pc.md#-t126--admm-req-yolo)
- **Notes**: W step = SGD on the loss + ρ/2‖W − Z + U‖²; Z step = projection of W + U;
  U ← U + W − Z; scheme (equidistant or powers of 2) chosen per layer
- **Done**: `admm.py` (`Trainer` hook, increasing ρ, `admm.csv`), validated on a quadratic
  problem. Run of 600 iterations (ρ 1e-3 → 0.023): **not converged** (residual 0.06 →
  0.08) and mAP 43.07, worse than the PTQ (fine-tuning without BN degraded the network, an
  unverified hypothesis) — `results/req_yolo.md`.
- **Remaining**: larger ρ, more iterations, per-layer plan; float mAP of the fine-tuning
  alone.
- **GPU**: optional (`--device gpu`, to add), see [T12.11](M12-profils-pc.md#-t1211--entraînement-sur-cpu-ou-gpu-au-choix)
