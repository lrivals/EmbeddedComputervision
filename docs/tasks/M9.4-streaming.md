# M9.4 — Streaming architecture

Goal: the second family of §10.1: one block per layer in a pipeline, a sliding window
produced by line buffers of (K−1)·W·C words
[2018-venieris#004.0, 2023-montgomerie-corcoran#011.0], compared with the single engine.

### [x] T9.4.1 — Resource and throughput model
- **Spec**: §10.1, §10.2 · **Depends on**: T8.1 · **Size**: M
- **Deliverables**: `tools/stream_model.py` + tests
- **Acceptance**: per layer: folding (PE × SIMD) under the DSP budget of the board, line
  buffers, weights on chip or in DDR, cycles; throughput = slowest layer; latency
- **Notes**: to check: do the 15.9 MB of int8 weights of Tiny-YOLOv2 fit on the KV260
  chip?
- **Done**: no (2.3 MB usable): hybrid, L12-L13 keep their input map and read their
  weights from DDR (13.5 MB/image). KV260 8-bit plan: II 6.23 M cycles, 32.1 img/s,
  latency ≈ 85 ms, 968 DSP; 4 bits: 64.2 img/s.
