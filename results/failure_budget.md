### Independence assumptions

- The three families add up (rare events, no common cause): a fault does not make the model miss more often than its own rate, and a hang does not coincide with a fault.
- The monitor coverage and the vote multiply: the vote acts on the runs the monitor did not flag as if they were a random sample of the runs.
- `model` is the **measured** burst rate (runs of 5 missed frames counted on video), not the product of per-frame misses: frame-to-frame correlation is inside it (×1,240 against independence, T27.4).
- A detected fault (ABFT), a flag of the monitor and a late frame are safe only once the fallback (T29.5) answers them with `UNSAFE`: the budget counts them as safe already, except the watchdog (`watchdog` = 0 until T29.5).
